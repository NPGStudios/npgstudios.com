Stowaway

Put your tabs away for later.

Stowaway saves your open tabs and tab groups so you can close them and bring them back later. It also keeps automatic snapshots to go back to after a crash or a stray click.

Coming soon to Chrome and Edge

What it does

Sessions you can go back to

Save all windows, one window, selected tabs or a single tab, with tab groups (names and colors), pinned tabs and window positions. Saving a session again keeps the earlier version, so you can step back.

Snapshots on their own

Every 15 minutes and shortly after your tabs change, Stowaway keeps a snapshot. All are kept for 48 hours, then one per hour for 7 days and one per day for 90 days. After a crash, it offers to reopen your last session.

Backup files on your own disk

Turn on backup files and Stowaway saves copies of everything to Downloads/Stowaway, outside the browser, and reads each one back to check it after it's written. A browser reset or reinstall leaves them in place.

Find any tab you saved

Search every saved tab, find and remove duplicate tabs in one step, with undo, and list sites that snapshots should skip, such as your bank or email.

Also included

  • Restores without disturbing you. Sessions open in new windows and leave what you have open alone. Big sessions load their tabs as you click them.
  • Bring your tabs over. Imports exports from OneTab, Session Buddy and Tab Session Manager, or a plain list of addresses.
  • Take them anywhere. Exports as a Stowaway backup (JSON), CSV, bookmarks (HTML), Markdown or one tab per line.
  • Trash. Deleted sessions stay in the Trash for 30 days.

Privacy

Stowaway keeps what it saves in your browser and, if you turn them on, in backup files in your Downloads folder. It reads the address, title and icon of your open tabs, and nothing from inside the pages. The Privacy Policy has the details.
